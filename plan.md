# Iron Gates trip – following Jondalar to the Sharamudoi

Inspired by Jean Auel's *The Valley of Horses*. According to Don's Maps, Auel
based the Sharamudoi on a mix of several sites in the Iron Gates
(Đerdap / Porțile de Fier) gorge, where the Danube cuts through the Carpathians
on the Serbian–Romanian border.

- **Who:** two couples, staying in guesthouses
- **Length:** 10–11 days
- **Getting around:** round trip by air to Belgrade (BEG), rental car, cycling for one day
- **When:** May–June or September (see "Season" below)

Optional extensions:

- **Romanian nature (+2 days):** Nera Gorge and the Beușnița and Bigăr waterfalls
  (1.5–2 h inland from Dubova)
- **Bulgaria (+1–2 days):** Belogradchik rocks and Magura cave (prehistoric paintings),
  reached from Kladovo / Negotin

## Day 8 – split group

**Cave group:** a guided Ponicova traverse (2–3 h, mud, helmet). Bring a headlamp
and clothes you don't mind getting dirty. Boat access depends on the water level,
and high water can close the cave.

**Non-cave group:** pick one or two of these, all within a few kilometres of Dubova:

- **Ciucarul Mare hike:** a short, steep climb (count on 2–3 h round trip)
  to the best viewpoint over the Cazanele Mari, chamois cliffs in Shamudoi style.
  It takes about as long as the cave visit.
- **Boat trip** to the Decebalus sculpture, Mraconia monastery and Tabula Traiana
  (the Roman inscription on the Serbian bank, best seen from the water).
  Boat trips also stop at **Veterani**, which is short, easy and dry compared with Ponicova.
- **Ciucarul Mic** or the Dubova bay shore: gentler walks with views back into the gorge.
- Swimming or a slow lunch on a terrace by the Danube in Dubova.

Meeting up afterwards: if the cave group exits on the river side, the boat
operator can bring them back to Dubova; agree the time and place in advance.

## Auel-related places

- **Lepenski Vir:** trapezoidal houses on a river terrace (the model for the
  Sharamudoi houses), fish-faced sculptures; 9500–6000 BC, so later than Jondalar's time
- **Iron Gates Mesolithic:** Vlasac, Padina, Hajdučka vodenica, Schela Cladovei;
  mostly flooded or with little visible on site, but covered by museums in Kladovo and Drobeta
- **Cuina Turcului (Dubova):** the region's only rock shelter;
  probably largely under the reservoir – check
- **Peștera cu Oase:** about 40,000-year-old modern human remains; closed to visitors
- **The Sister = Tisza:** joins the Danube near Titel (day 2)

## Season

- **May–June:** green and lush, long days. Spring high water may close Ponicova or block boat entry.
- **September:** warm water, lower river (better cave access), grape harvest, fewer people.
  Veterani is open May–October.
- July–August: up to 35 °C; caves and the river help, but hiking is hot.

## Practicalities

- **Rental car – important:** at least Enterprise in Belgrade *excludes Romania*
  (along with Bulgaria, Kosovo and Albania). Ask several companies, including local
  Belgrade agencies, for written permission for Romania ≥ 7 working days before pickup
  (expect ≈ €60 for the green card / cross-border fee). Fallback: leave the car in
  Kladovo and get a taxi or guesthouse transfer across the dam to Dubova (≈ 50 km).
  Boats from Dubova cover the Romanian sights, so you hardly need a car there.
  (Check whether taxis can cross the border.)
- **Mobile:** Serbia is not in the EU, so there's no EU roaming; buy a local SIM or eSIM.
- **Currency:** Serbian dinar (RSD) and Romanian leu (RON); carry some cash for boats and guides.
- **Bikes (day 6):** few rental options in Golubac / Donji Milanovac; book ahead.
  The gorge road has unlit tunnels, so bring lights.
- **Guesthouses:** roughly €30–60 per room per night.
